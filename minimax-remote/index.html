MiniMax Remote

MiniMax Remote is an early preview. The iPhone app pairs over your local network to a companion process on your Mac, and the app itself is real — but the sessions, agents, and chat replies it shows are placeholder data, not your actual work. The connection to your real agent runtime is not built yet.

Availability

  • iOSBeta awaiting Apple review
Support

What works today

  • Local-network pairing. The iPhone pairs to the Mac companion by QR code or a one-time token, and the app talks to it over HTTP on your LAN. This part is real and working.
  • The iOS app. The full dashboard UI — chat, sessions, crons, agents, drive — is implemented and renders against the companion.

What is not real yet

  • Session and agent data. The sessions, crons, agents, and drive listings you see are hardcoded sample data written by the companion, not your MiniMax Code sessions.
  • Chat replies. Streaming replies and tool-call output are canned, not generated by an agent.
  • Connection to your real agent runtime. The companion does not yet read from your live MiniMax Code sessions; that integration is the next build.

Scope and data

The MiniMax Remote source repository is private.  Nothing you do in the app reaches your real agent sessions or files — all data shown is sample content. Current pairing and network requirements should be confirmed with the beta build.

See the product support page and privacy notice for more detail.
