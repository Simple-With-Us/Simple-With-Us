Socratic Trade

Socratic Trade is a web trading workspace for supported broker accounts.  Review proposals and the app's approval controls before placing an order.

Availability

  • WebWeb app ↗
  • iOSInvite-only beta in preparation ↗
Support

What you can do

  • Broker connection. Connect a supported account after sign-in.
  • Proposal review. Inspect the proposed action and its context.
  • Execution. Use the app's controls to manage orders with your broker.
