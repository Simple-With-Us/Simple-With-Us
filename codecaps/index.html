CodeCaps

Keep AI quota and reset windows in view from the macOS menu bar.  CodeCaps is engineered with the proactive safeguards you'll come to rely on — detecting runaway usage and anomalies, graphing local usage history, and surfacing an adaptive Picture in Picture (PiP) window when your quota runs low.

What you can do

  • Runaway usage and anomaly detection. Evaluates quota burn against your 7-day rolling average and peak-hour baseline.  Catches stuck loops and unexpected spikes before your daily or weekly quota is exhausted.
  • Usage history and trend graphs. Visualizes local quota drain over time with correlated anomaly alert markers directly on your timeline.
  • Low-quota Picture in Picture (PiP). Keeps high-priority quotas visible in an on-top floating micro-HUD.  PiP can automatically open whenever any provider's quota runs low, keeping you informed without interrupting your workflow.
  • Multi-cadence quotas and smart reset alerts. Monitors rapid 5-hour pacing alongside weekly and monthly plan allowances, with reset alarms that notify on period rollovers.
  • Zero typed credentials. Reuses existing local CLI and session logins for Claude, Codex, Cursor, Antigravity, Grok, MiniMax, and Muse rather than requiring provider API keys.
