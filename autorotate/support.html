Support

For a rotation issue, include the connector name, rotation ID, and outcome without sending secret material.

Before contacting us

A rotation can be partial after one destination updates and another fails.  Inspect the affected target, reconnect if necessary, then run a new rotation.

Check the product page for current platform availability and the privacy notice for site-level information.

Contact

Email feedback@simplewithus.com with your device or browser version and steps to reproduce.  Leave out passwords, API keys, personal contact data, and private logs.
