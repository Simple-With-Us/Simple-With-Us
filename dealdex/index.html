DealDex

DealDex brings available listing and pricing sources into one web view.  Search for a card or inspect a listing, then check source and freshness before buying.

What you can do

  • Search listings. Explore available eBay and Mercari results.
  • Compare prices. Read the market sources returned for that card and condition.
  • Decide yourself. Source coverage and freshness vary; purchases take place on the marketplace.

Scope and data

Pricing data is an estimate based on available sources.  The product site contains the current web workflow and access details.
