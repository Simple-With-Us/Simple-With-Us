Public disclosure explorer

Explore public trading disclosures.

Congress.Trade organizes public filings and ranked views.  Filter by person, company, and date, then open the underlying source before drawing conclusions.

What you can do

  • Find filings. Browse House, Senate, and available executive-branch disclosures.
  • Read dates carefully. A transaction date, disclosure date, and ingestion date can differ.
  • Ranked views. The site offers rankings; review the published methodology and source data.

Scope and data

Disclosures can arrive well after a transaction.  The site does not establish a trading advantage or predict future performance.
