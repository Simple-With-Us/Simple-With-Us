HogHunter

Track CPU and memory hogs on your Mac, clean up disk clutter, and check or control it all from your iPhone.

Availability

  • macOSDownload themacOS Beta
  • iOSJoin the beta onApple TestFlight
Contact

What it does

  • Names the apps and processes eating CPU and memory right now, over the past hour, and over the past 24 hours - then quit or force quit a hog after a confirm.
  • Shows what is using your disk, down to each app's hidden costs (containers, caches, logs, saved state), and cleans reclaimable clutter with a Standard or deeper Extreme clean.
  • Maintain runs scheduled background cleaning and maintenance scripts, with run history and alerts when a schedule is missed.
  • Watches live network bandwidth with a 24-hour peak.
  • Alerts when an app holds CPU above your threshold for a sustained time, optionally to a webhook.
  • iPhone companion over local Wi-Fi or Tailscale: see the same lists, quit or tame apps, run cleans, and change settings, with every action confirmed on the phone. Home-screen widgets included.
