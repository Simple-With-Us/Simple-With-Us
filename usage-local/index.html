Usage Monitor Local

Track subscriptions and supported provider usage on your iPhone, without setting up a Usage Monitor server.

Availability

  • iOSJoin the beta onApple TestFlight

What you can do

  • Direct polling. Supported adapters include selected provider cost or balance endpoints; coverage varies by provider.
  • Manual rows. Enter subscription charges that providers do not expose through an API.
  • Local data. Provider polling sends requests to those providers; stored tracking data remains on the device unless you choose to export it.

Scope and data

The app does not require the Usage Monitor server.  Direct polling needs network access and provider credentials for the chosen adapters.

See the support section below and the privacy notice for more detail.

Support

For a missing reading, note the provider adapter, last refresh time, and whether you entered a manual subscription row.

  • Before you write. Direct polling uses provider credentials from iOS Keychain and makes network requests to supported services.  Subscription rows may need manual updates.
  • Contact. Write to contact@simplewithus.com with your iPhone version and steps to reproduce.  Leave out passwords, API keys, personal contact data, and private logs.
